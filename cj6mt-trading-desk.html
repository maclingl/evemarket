Click a row for price history, the local order book and your cost. The tabs cover Tech I capital modules (no rigs, no named meta variants, nothing where a rare BPC is most of the cost), T1 ships and T2 ships, whether you own them or not. Highlighted prices are below what the item reprocesses for, using the yield and prices in Settings. Hide any you don't trade with "Stop watching"; Settings can restore them. Orders on other items aren't tracked. Price history is region-wide, so it includes any other markets in the region. Corporation hangars and corporation orders are not included. The structure market refreshes about every 5 minutes on CCP's side.
